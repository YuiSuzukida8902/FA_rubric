  <h1>Evaluation Criteria</h1>

  <p>There are four evaluation criteria for this task:</p>

  <ul>

    <li>1. Content</li>

    <li>2. Task Requirement</li>

    <li>3. Coherence and cohesion</li>

    <li>4. Comprehensibility</li>

  </ul>

  <p>Please refer to the descriptions below and use them as a basis for your judgments.</p>


  <section>

    <h2>1. Content</h2>

    <p><strong>Is the number of information units provided in the text adequate and relevant?</strong></p>

    <table>

      <tr><th class="rating">Rating</th><th>Description</th></tr>

      <tr><td>1</td><td>The number of ideas is not at all adequate and insufficient and the ideas are unrelated to each other.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>2</td><td>The number of ideas is scarcely adequate, the ideas lack consistency.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>3</td><td>The number of ideas is somewhat adequate, even though they are not very consistent.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>4</td><td>The number of ideas is adequate and they are sufficiently consistent.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>5</td><td>The number of ideas is very adequate, they are very consistent to each other.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>6</td><td>The number of ideas is extremely adequate and they are very consistent to each other.</td></tr>

    </table>

  </section>


  <section>

    <h2>2. Task Requirements</h2>

    <p><strong>Have the task requirements been fulfilled successfully (e.g. genre, speech acts, register)?</strong></p>

    <table>

      <tr><th class="rating">Rating</th><th>Description</th></tr>

      <tr><td>1</td><td>None of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>2</td><td>Some (less than half) of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>3</td><td>Approximately half of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>4</td><td>Most (more than half) of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>5</td><td>Almost all of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>6</td><td>All of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.</td></tr>

    </table>

  </section>


  <section>

    <h2>3. Coherence and Cohesion</h2>

    <p><strong>Is the text coherent and cohesive (e.g. cohesive devices, strategies)?</strong></p>

    <table>

      <tr><th class="rating">Rating</th><th>Description</th></tr>

      <tr>

        <td>1</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is not at all coherent. Unrelated progressions and coherence breaks are very common.<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> The speaker does not use any anaphoric device.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> The speech is not at all cohesive. Connectives are hardly ever used, and ideas are unrelated.

        </td>

      </tr>

      <tr>

        <td>2</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is scarcely coherent. The speaker often uses unrelated progressions; coherence is occasionally achieved through repetitions.<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> Only a few anaphoric devices are used.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> There are some coherence breaks. The speech is not very cohesive. Ideas are not well linked by connectives, which are rarely used.

        </td>

      </tr>

      <tr>

        <td>3</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is somewhat coherent. Unrelated progressions and/or repetitions are frequent. More than two sentences in a row may have the same subject (even if understood).<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> Some anaphoric devices are used.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> There may be a few coherence breaks. The speech is somewhat cohesive, mostly using conjunctions as connectives.

        </td>

      </tr>

      <tr>

        <td>4</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is coherent. Unrelated progressions are somewhat rare, though repetitions are sometimes used to maintain coherence.<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> A sufficient number of anaphoric devices is used.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> The performance is cohesive. The speaker makes good use of connectives, sometimes using phrases beyond basic conjunctions.

        </td>

      </tr>

      <tr>

        <td>5</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is very coherent. New topics are usually introduced with connectives or connective phrases. Repetitions are infrequent.<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> Anaphoric devices are numerous.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> There are no coherence breaks. The performance is very cohesive, with adverbial and/or verbal connectives linking ideas well.

        </td>

      </tr>

      <tr>

        <td>6</td>

        <td>

          <strong>Coherence:</strong> The performance is extremely coherent. New ideas are smoothly integrated with connectives or connective phrases. There are few unrelated progressions and no coherence breaks.<br>

          <strong>Anaphoric Devices:</strong> Anaphoric devices are regularly used.<br>

          <strong>Cohesion:</strong> The structure is extremely cohesive, using a wide range of connectives (linking chunks, verbal constructions, and adverbials) to clearly express relationships between ideas.

        </td>

      </tr>

    </table>

  </section>


  <section>

    <h2>4. Comprehensibility</h2>

    <p><strong>How much effort is required to understand text purpose and ideas?</strong></p>

    <table>

      <tr><th class="rating">Rating</th><th>Description</th></tr>

      <tr><td>1</td><td>The performance is not at all comprehensible. Ideas and purposes are unclearly stated, and the listener's efforts to understand the text are ineffective.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>2</td><td>The performance is scarcely comprehensible. Purposes are not clearly stated, and the listener struggles to understand the speaker’s ideas. The listener must guess most ideas and purposes.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>3</td><td>The performance is somewhat comprehensible. Some sentences are hard to understand on first listen; a second attempt clarifies purposes and ideas, though some doubts persist.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>4</td><td>The performance is comprehensible. Only a few sentences are unclear but can be understood, without much effort, after a second listening.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>5</td><td>The performance is easily comprehensible and flows smoothly. Comprehensibility is not an issue.</td></tr>

      <tr><td>6</td><td>The performance is very easily comprehensible and highly fluent. Ideas and purpose are clearly stated.</td></tr>

    </table>

  </section>
