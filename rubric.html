Evaluation Criteria

There are four evaluation criteria for this task:

  • 1. Content
  • 2. Task Requirement
  • 3. Coherence and cohesion
  • 4. Comprehensibility

Please refer to the descriptions below and use them as a basis for your judgments.

1. Content

Is the number of information units provided in the text adequate and relevant?

RatingDescription
1The number of ideas is not at all adequate and insufficient and the ideas are unrelated to each other.
2The number of ideas is scarcely adequate, the ideas lack consistency.
3The number of ideas is somewhat adequate, even though they are not very consistent.
4The number of ideas is adequate and they are sufficiently consistent.
5The number of ideas is very adequate, they are very consistent to each other.
6The number of ideas is extremely adequate and they are very consistent to each other.

2. Task Requirements

Have the task requirements been fulfilled successfully (e.g. genre, speech acts, register)?

RatingDescription
1None of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.
2Some (less than half) of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.
3Approximately half of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.
4Most (more than half) of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.
5Almost all of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.
6All of the questions and requirements of the task have been answered.

3. Coherence and Cohesion

Is the text coherent and cohesive (e.g. cohesive devices, strategies)?

RatingDescription
1 Coherence: The performance is not at all coherent. Unrelated progressions and coherence breaks are very common.
Anaphoric Devices: The speaker does not use any anaphoric device.
Cohesion: The speech is not at all cohesive. Connectives are hardly ever used, and ideas are unrelated.
2 Coherence: The performance is scarcely coherent. The speaker often uses unrelated progressions; coherence is occasionally achieved through repetitions.
Anaphoric Devices: Only a few anaphoric devices are used.
Cohesion: There are some coherence breaks. The speech is not very cohesive. Ideas are not well linked by connectives, which are rarely used.
3 Coherence: The performance is somewhat coherent. Unrelated progressions and/or repetitions are frequent. More than two sentences in a row may have the same subject (even if understood).
Anaphoric Devices: Some anaphoric devices are used.
Cohesion: There may be a few coherence breaks. The speech is somewhat cohesive, mostly using conjunctions as connectives.
4 Coherence: The performance is coherent. Unrelated progressions are somewhat rare, though repetitions are sometimes used to maintain coherence.
Anaphoric Devices: A sufficient number of anaphoric devices is used.
Cohesion: The performance is cohesive. The speaker makes good use of connectives, sometimes using phrases beyond basic conjunctions.
5 Coherence: The performance is very coherent. New topics are usually introduced with connectives or connective phrases. Repetitions are infrequent.
Anaphoric Devices: Anaphoric devices are numerous.
Cohesion: There are no coherence breaks. The performance is very cohesive, with adverbial and/or verbal connectives linking ideas well.
6 Coherence: The performance is extremely coherent. New ideas are smoothly integrated with connectives or connective phrases. There are few unrelated progressions and no coherence breaks.
Anaphoric Devices: Anaphoric devices are regularly used.
Cohesion: The structure is extremely cohesive, using a wide range of connectives (linking chunks, verbal constructions, and adverbials) to clearly express relationships between ideas.

4. Comprehensibility

How much effort is required to understand text purpose and ideas?

RatingDescription
1The performance is not at all comprehensible. Ideas and purposes are unclearly stated, and the listener's efforts to understand the text are ineffective.
2The performance is scarcely comprehensible. Purposes are not clearly stated, and the listener struggles to understand the speaker’s ideas. The listener must guess most ideas and purposes.
3The performance is somewhat comprehensible. Some sentences are hard to understand on first listen; a second attempt clarifies purposes and ideas, though some doubts persist.
4The performance is comprehensible. Only a few sentences are unclear but can be understood, without much effort, after a second listening.
5The performance is easily comprehensible and flows smoothly. Comprehensibility is not an issue.
6The performance is very easily comprehensible and highly fluent. Ideas and purpose are clearly stated.
